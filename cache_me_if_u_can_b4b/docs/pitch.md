## Slide 2. The root causes

**Message:** The problem is not ability. It is fear and missing practice.

- Fear of making a mistake that cannot be undone
- Fear of looking foolish in front of family
- Words they were never taught ("Verify OTP", "Clear Cache", "Allow Access", "Storage Full": their exact words)
- No safe place to practise
- Help that does the task for them instead of teaching

Speaker notes:
- Both people we spoke to said family takes the phone and does it for them, and both stop and wait when stuck.
- Most help today is "give me the phone". It solves today and teaches nothing.
- Mashq goes after the fear and the missing practice, not the person.

---

## Slide 3. The solution loop

**Message:** Try, Surprise, Teach: safe practice that builds real independence.

- **Try:** a practice form on a fictional "Citizen Services Portal (Practice)". Nothing is real.
- **Surprise:** eight planted hiccups (logged out, wrong date shape, photo rejected, a verification code) and one calm habit for each.
- **Teach:** the parent advises a confused neighbour. Explaining it is how it sticks.
- Then check, submit, and see where they are on the ladder: with the guide, with a little help, on my own.

Speaker notes:
- Real forms surprise people, so we plant the surprises where it is safe.
- A wrong tap never blocks; it shows what would happen and how to fix it.
- No timers, no scores, no red crosses. Urdu first, with voice.

## Slide 5. Trust and privacy

**Message:** Safe by design: nothing leaves the phone, and nothing is real.

- Progress stays on the phone.
- The Guide explains form boxes on web forms. It never fills anything in and never reads what is typed.
- Practice data is fictional (the Practice Person is made up).
- No real organisation names or logos. Safety lines never use scare words.

Speaker notes:
- The people we build for are the most targeted by tricks. We do not add to the risk.
- The helper never asks for a code, PIN or password, and says so.
- Everything works offline except optional live video search.
